What is TWI?

TWI Solutions is a community focused on helping young Africans build practical skills and access opportunities.

Is it free to enroll?

Our programmes and enrollment details will provide information about available fees and opportunities.

Who can join?

Young Africans who are interested in learning, growing and developing practical skills can join our community.

What skill can I learn?

You can explore the different skills and programmes available on TWI Solutions.

How do I register?

Click the registration button on our website and follow the instructions to get started.

Do I need previous experience?

No, beginners are welcome to explore our programmes.

How long are the programmes?

Programme duration depends on the learning track.

Will I receive a certificate?

Check the details of your selected programme for certification information.
